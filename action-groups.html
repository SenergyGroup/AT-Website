What is meeting now.

Advancing Together currently convenes around Economic Stability and helps coordinate the larger CHIP effort. Housing Stability and Transportation Challenges remain part of the process through partner-led groups already carrying that work.

Not every CHIP priority uses the same meeting structure. Advancing Together is the main table moving the Economic Stability work and coordinating across priorities. Transportation and Housing stay connected through external community groups, so this page points people to the right lane instead of duplicating their full agendas.

Economic Stability Active

Advancing Together convenings

This is the active Advancing Together table. It carries the Economic Stability priority while also helping coordinate with the Housing Stability and Transportation Challenges work happening through other community partners.

Goal To create a coordinated and inclusive community environment where all have opportunities for stable employment, income, and well-being through systems, policies, and partnerships that reduce economic barriers and strengthen participation in decision-making.
  • Hosts quarterly convenings to share progress, strengthen relationships, and identify emerging economic stability issues.
  • Focuses on systems change, partner coordination, resource navigation, and lived-experience leadership.
  • Serves as the place where Economic Stability work stays connected to the other two CHIP priorities.

§ 01 Economic Stability at AT

The active Advancing Together work is centered on Economic Stability.

The CHIP one-pager groups the Economic Stability strategies into three buckets: changing the rules, strengthening partner collaboration, and supporting individuals. That is the clearest picture of what Advancing Together is carrying right now.

1–4

Changing the Rules

  1. Advocate for local policies and programs that reduce the cost burden of essential needs.
  2. Expand lived-experience leadership so community members most affected by economic barriers shape decisions and are compensated for their time.
  3. Promote rapid-response approaches such as microgrants, events, or policy recommendations when new issues emerge.
  4. Build a collaborative resource navigation system so people can enter through any community “door” and still reach aligned supports.
5–11

Community and Partner Collaboration

  1. Explore sustainable funding mechanisms to strengthen coalition infrastructure and Economic Stability strategies.
  2. Align with Health Sector CHIP objectives that reduce long-term household financial strain through prevention, screenings, and behavioral health initiatives.
  3. Host quarterly Advancing Together convenings to strengthen relationships, share progress, and identify emerging issues.
  4. Develop a community data hub that centralizes reliable, place-based data.
  5. Expand navigator and liaison capacity for community members facing language, cultural, or logistical barriers.
  6. Strengthen the ecosystem between navigators, employers, employees, and workforce agencies.
  7. Educate community organizations on practices that improve the accessibility of public-facing materials.
12–13

Supporting Individuals

  1. Promote community employment and resource fairs that connect people to job opportunities, training programs, and wraparound supports.
  2. Promote compensation policies that remove financial and structural barriers for community members contributing lived experience.
How It Fits Together

One CHIP roadmap, three priorities, and different tables carrying different parts of the work.

Shared process

Economic Stability, Housing Stability, and Transportation Challenges are all part of the same community health improvement process.

Different meeting homes

Advancing Together actively carries Economic Stability, while Housing and Transportation stay aligned through existing external partner groups.
