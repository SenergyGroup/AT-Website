Join the work.

The strongest part of Advancing Together is the people who show up for it. Whether you want updates a few times a year or you're ready to dig in with an action group, we'd love to hear from you.

Why join

Stay connected to community health improvement in the Cedar Valley.

Advancing Together is a coalition of 40+ partners working across Economic Stability, Housing Stability, and Transportation Challenges. Joining our email list is the easiest way to hear about meetings, action groups, and moments where your voice or expertise can help move the work forward.

  • Email list. Occasional updates when there's something worth sharing — meetings, opportunities to weigh in, and calls for action groups.
  • Bi-yearly newsletter. A twice-a-year digest of progress across the three CHIP priority areas, with stories from partners and residents.
  • Action groups. If you want to help organize on a specific priority, check the box below and we'll be in touch when action groups are forming.
  • No spam, ever. You can unsubscribe any time — every email includes a one-click link.
